## 🔮 The Vision

For decades, creating high-stakes documents forced an impossible compromise:

* Either struggle with arcane markup languages, cryptic compiler errors, and endless packaging conflicts just to get clean typography...
* Or surrender to sluggish, unpredictable word processors where moving an image 2 millimeters sends your entire layout across three pages.

**Typst Studio changes that.**

We believe document creation should feel like sculpting in a visual canvas while retaining the uncompromising precision, typographic hierarchy, and sub-second rendering of modern typesetting engines.

Whether you are an engineer writing a technical whitepaper, a founder preparing an executive brief, a researcher publishing formulas, or a designer assembling an editorial spread, Typst Studio provides a fluid canvas that respects your time and elevates your work.

### 💫 Designed for Flow
* **Direct Visual Composition**: Drag, drop, group, and arrange cards, media, callouts, and multi-track grids with zero friction.
* **Instant Parity Preview**: What you see on your editing canvas is what prints in your final PDF. No guesswork, no shifting margins.
* **Color Harmony at a Click**: Transform the emotional tone of your publication instantaneously with hand-crafted document palettes—from *Editorial Emerald* and *Classic Blue* to *Warm Amber* and *Crimson Velvet*.
* **Editorial-Grade Storytelling**: Enrich your narrative with dedicated pull quotes, numbered milestone timelines, key-value specification sheets, metric progress meters, and formal signature blocks.
* **Typographic Rigor**: Built upon the next-generation Typst engine, giving you razor-sharp micro-typography, hyphenation, Ligature support, and vector clarity at any scale.

## 🗺️ The Horizon: What We Are Building Next

Typst Studio is an evolving canvas. Here is our roadmap for the future of document engineering:

### 🧠 Generative Document Intelligence
Natural language layout synthesis. Describe what you want (*"Format this project post-mortem into a two-column executive summary with key metrics at the top and a sign-off at the bottom"*), and watch your document compose itself live.

### 👥 Real-Time Multiplayer Collaboration
Write, design, and refine together in real time with teammates. Experience presence awareness, cursor tracking, and collaborative commenting without ever leaving your canvas.

### 🌐 Universal Multi-Format Publishing
Write once, publish everywhere. Export your visual documents not only as vector PDFs, but simultaneously as interactive web publications, responsive ePub digital books, and high-resolution presentation slides.

### 🧩 The Community Template Galaxy
A global showcase and marketplace for designers, universities, and organizations to publish, share, and fork beautiful document templates.

### 📊 Visual Data & Diagramming Suite
Directly embed reactive charts, interactive data tables, and native vector diagrams into your document layouts without opening third-party tools.

## ⚡ Try It in 60 Seconds

Typst Studio is fully containerized and runs anywhere with Docker:

```bash
# Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/Abdo30004/typst-studio.git
cd typst-studio

# Launch your studio
docker compose up -d
```

Open **[http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000)** in your browser and start creating.

## 🤝 Philosophy & Community

Typst Studio is **free, open-source, and local-first**:
* **Privacy First**: Your documents, data, and assets remain on your machine or private server.
* **No Lock-In**: Inspect, copy, or export the pure Typst source markup at any second.
* **Built for Longevity**: Open standards, clean JSON document schemas, and portable artifacts.

Contributions, feature requests, and feedback are warmly welcomed. Join us in shaping the future of documents.
